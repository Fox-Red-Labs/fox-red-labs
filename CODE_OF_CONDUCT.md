# Code of conduct

This is a bench for people learning to make small chips do things. Everyone here was a beginner at something this year.

- Be kind. Assume the other person is trying.
- Argue with measurements, not adjectives. "My board took 9 minutes" beats "this is slow".
- No vendor trash talk, no personal attacks, no harassment of any kind, anywhere the project is discussed.
- Say when you are not sure. "Suspected" is a fine word.
- Credit people for their work and their reports.

If someone breaks this, write to hello@foxredlabs.dev. Reports are read by one person and kept private. The response can be a word, a removal of the content, or a block from the project, depending on what happened.

This text is short on purpose. Where it is silent, the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>) applies.
