Button, knob and dimmer

The steps, and what happens inside the chip. Open the build page

Wiring. Knob wiper to J1 pin 2 (PA14), its ends to J1 pin 1 (3V3) and J2 pin 20 (GND). The LED is LED2 green on the board, J3 pin 28 (PB27); an outside LED through 330 Ω works there too. S2 is on the board, J4 pin 40 (PB3); optional: your own button on J2 pin 15 (PB8), other side to GND.
