IWRL6432BOOST (IWRL6432)

The IWRL6432 is a radar sensor in TI's mmWave family: 160 MHz Arm Cortex-M4F, 2 MB of flash, 1 MB of RAM, 57 to 64 GHz radar, 2 TX, 3 RX, low power.
