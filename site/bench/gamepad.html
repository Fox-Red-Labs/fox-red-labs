Gamepad Reader

Press a button on a paired Bluetooth gamepad and watch it light here. No gamepad? Replay the capture from the LP-EM-CC2340R5.

No gamepad yet. Pair one, then press any button.
  1. Build and flash the live gamepad: LIVE=1 bash ports/esp32-ble-gamepad/boards/cc2340r5/build.sh, then flash the .hex without verify.
  2. On the computer, open Bluetooth settings, pair CC2340R5 Gamepad and accept the pairing prompt.
  3. Open this page in Chrome or Edge, press any button once so the browser hands the gamepad to the page, then press BTN-1 and BTN-2.
