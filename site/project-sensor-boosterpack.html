Six sensors on one BoosterPack: what you get, what is read, how accurate it is

You need

  • LP-MSPM0G5187 LaunchPad development kit: TI's 80 MHz Cortex-M0+ board with a TinyEngine neural accelerator, 128 kB flash and 32 kB RAM. The chip has USB and I2S audio. The board adds a microphone, an audio ADC, a microSD slot and a debugger that measures current.
  • BP-BASSENSORSMKII Sensors BoosterPack plug-in module: A plug-on board of TI and Bosch sensors on one I2C bus. The TMP117 reads temperature and sits on a break-away board with a ribbon cable. The HDC2080 reads temperature and humidity, the OPT3001 light, the BMI160 motion, with a BMM150 compass behind it, and the DRV5055 a magnet. Red and green LEDs.

Wiring

  1. No wires and no jumpers: press the BoosterPack straight down until its pin 1 (3V3) sits in the LaunchPad kit's J1 pin 1, with the USB cable unplugged
  2. The program switches the sensors on: pin 6 (PB22) low for humidity, pin 24 (PB15) low for the magnet sensor, pin 27 (PA15) high for light, pin 28 (PA12) high for the TMP117
  3. I2C on pins 9 (PA9, clock) and 10 (PA8, data); the magnet sensor's analog output on pin 2 (PA25)
  4. TMP117 only: the flat cable from J5 to J6, contacts down, black tab up; it only works one way round, and the wrong way kills the I2C bus (the TMP117 did not answer in any fit)
  5. Only the J1 and J3 header rows have pins on this LaunchPad, so BoosterPack pins 1 to 10 and 21 to 30 are connected
  6. The two LEDs (pins 39 and 40) and the motion sensor's second interrupt (pin 13) are not; the other alert lines are connected but the program does not read them

What was measured

  • Humidity and temperature (HDC2080): 24.3 to 24.5 C and 37.4 to 37.8 percent RH; the data sheet says within 2 percent RH and 0.2 C typical
  • Light (OPT3001): 296 to 319 lux in the bench light; the data sheet gives 1800 to 2200 for a 2000 lux input and no figure at 300 lux
  • Magnet sensor (DRV5055A4): 1.64 V with no magnet, against 1.65 V at rest in the data sheet (1.59 to 1.71 V guaranteed), assuming a 3.3 V ADC reference
  • TMP117 (flat cable tried in every fit, also with 3.3 V wired to its power pin) and the motion sensor (BMI160, fitted but silent): no readings, cause not found for either
  • No reference instrument was used, so these are not accuracy tests; the accuracy figures are the data sheets', at the conditions the guide lists

Versions: MSPM0 SDK 2.11.00.07, TI Arm Clang 5.1.1, SysConfig 1.26.3, MSPM0 G5187 Rev. A; run 2026-10-10.

Next: Try a second flat cable or the BoosterPack on another LaunchPad to see whether the TMP117 and the BMI160 answer there, then cover the light sensor and bring a magnet to the DRV5055. The light reading also feeds an occupancy model on the chip's neural accelerator..
