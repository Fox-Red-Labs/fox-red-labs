LP-MSPM0G5187 (MSPM0G5187)

The MSPM0G5187 is a microcontroller in TI's MSPM0 family: 80 MHz Arm Cortex-M0+, 128 kB of flash, 32 kB of RAM.

Get it running

First blink: 10.1 s from plug-in to a changed blink (terminal; 3 runs).

  1. Install CCStudio 21.0.1 and the MSPM0 SDK 2.11. Free with a myTI login. On a Mac, mac-setup/bootstrap.sh in the repository does both after you sign in.

  2. File, Import Project, and pick:

    /Applications/ti/mspm0_sdk_2_11_00_07/examples/nortos/LP_MSPM0G5187/driverlib/gpio_toggle_output

  3. Click the green Debug button, then Resume.

    You should see: an LED on the board blinks. The pattern is the delay in gpio_toggle_output.c: change it, build and flash again, and the blink changes.

  1. Copy TI's blink example and build it.

    cp -R <SDK>/examples/nortos/LP_MSPM0G5187/driverlib/gpio_toggle_output . ../mspm0g3507/mcu-bench/sdk-common/sdkmake.sh gpio_toggle_output/ticlang

  2. Flash it with UniFlash's command line.

    <UniFlash>/dslite.sh --mode flash -c MSPM0G5187_<serial>.ccxml -e -f -v -u gpio_toggle_output/ticlang/gpio_toggle_output.out

Replace the parts in angle brackets. Run it from the terminal in VS Code, CCStudio or any IDE.

You should see: an LED on the board blinks. The pattern is the delay in gpio_toggle_output.c: change it, build and flash again, and the blink changes.

  • Interrupt latency, pend to first handler line: 36 cycles (bare metal; 80 MHz; 3 runs)

  • Room-occupancy AI model, one inference: 1.292 ms (-O2; 80 MHz; 3 runs)

Ports and wiring demos

  • Arduino display libraries on the LP-MSPM0G3507, and the core layer on the LP-MSPM0G5187 Works: Arduino_GFX's graphics test draws on an ST7796S screen on the MSPM0 G3507, through a 649-line Arduino API layer under the unmodified libraries.
  • Fixed-weight DSP (FIR, filter bank, DFT) on the LP-MSPM0G5187 Works: A 64-tap FIR filter runs on the MSPM0 G5187's NPU (its neural-network accelerator), compiled with NNC, TI's neural-network compiler, 29.7 times faster than the same graph on the CPU, with byte-for-byte equal output.
  • MicroPython 1.29.0 on the LP-MSPM0G3519, LP-MSPM0G3507 and LP-MSPM0G5187 Works: A new MicroPython port boots on the MSPM0 G3519 with the MicroPython banner and drives a 3.5 inch touch screen from Python.
  • Peanut-GB Game Boy emulator on MSPM0 and MSPM33 Works: Peanut-GB plays Tobu Tobu Girl on the MSPM33 C321A with the Educational BoosterPack screen at the console's own speed.
  • Post-quantum key exchange and signatures on seven boards: LP-CC1354P10-1, LP-EM-CC2340R5, LP-AM243, LP-MSPM0G3519, LP-MSPM33C321A, LP-EM-CC2755P10 and LP-MSPM0G5187 Works: ML-KEM-768 key exchange and ML-DSA-44 signatures pass NIST's known-answer vectors on seven boards, from a 36 kB Cortex-M0+ to an 800 MHz Cortex-R5F; the CC1354P10-1 also agrees a key with a Mac.
  • STM32 UART DMA receive with idle-line interrupt (MaJerle stm32-usart-uart-dma-rx-tx) on TI boards Works: MaJerle's STM32 UART DMA example answers every test packet at 115200 baud on the MSPM0 G3519, G5187, L1306, C1104 and MSPM33C321A.
  • Sub-1 GHz CC1407P radio on an LP-MSPM0G5187, heard by an LP-CC1354P10-1 Works: TI's CC1407P sub-1 GHz radio driver runs unchanged on the MSPM0 G5187, and a CC1354P10-1 receives its packets: 21,658 of 21,658.
  • uLisp Lisp interpreter on the LP-MSPM0G5187, LP-MSPM0G3507, LP-MSPM0G3519, LP-EM-CC2340R5, LP-EM-CC2755P10, LP-MSPM33C321A, LP-AM243 and LAUNCHXL-F2800137 Works: uLisp, an Arduino sketch, gives a Lisp prompt on the serial port of the MSPM0 G5187, CC2340R5 and CC2755P10: 19 of 19 REPL (type-and-run loop) checks pass on each.
  • Arduboy2-compatible layer and three open-source games on MSPM0 and MSPM33 Could work: Snake, TileWorld and Crate Confusion build unchanged against an Arduboy2-compatible layer for four kits, and Snake draws on the MSPM33 C321A's Educational BoosterPack screen.
  • BoosterPack drivers (Educational MKII, Sharp128, Audio, Sensors MKII, NPU DSP) on LaunchPad kits of other families Could work: Portable C cores for BoosterPacks ran: the Educational pack on the MSPM0 L1306 and MSPM33 C321A, the Sharp128 panel on the MSPM0 C1104, the Audio pack on the AM13E230 and the Sensors pack on the MSPM0 G5187. Fixes

  • If you see "OK Kilby" never lights the LED, stack a BOOSTXL-EDUMKII, or use the I2S port in mspm0g5187/wakeword/ (its microphone run needs the radio wires off header J20).
  • If you see the program waits forever on tvmgen_default_finished, flash with DSLite so the image starts itself, then halt and read without a reset.
  • If you see adc12_monitor_supply reads codes of 540 to 674 that drift, do not use this example to read the supply on this board; the G3507 reads a steady 1,796 to 1,797.
  • If you see Debugger shows SYSCFG_DL_SYSCTL_CLK_init and nothing happens after a good flash, skip those examples.
  • If you see GCONV layer NOT offloaded to TI-NPU, accept it.
  • If you see scan=none from the Sensors BoosterPack, unplug USB, press the BoosterPack straight down so its pin 1 (3V3) sits in J1 pin 1, replug.
  • If you see tmp117=none with the ribbon cable in, try a second cable or another LaunchPad.
  • If you see I2C scan shows every address from 08 to 77 after plugging the flat cable, unplug USB, turn the cable around, push both ends in and press the latches flat.
