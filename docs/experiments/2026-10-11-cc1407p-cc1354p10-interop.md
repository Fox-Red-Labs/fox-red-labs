# Can TI's CC1407P and CC1354P10 talk to each other on every protocol they share?

**Short answer.** Packets went both ways on nine of the eleven protocol pairs tried, and seven of them were clean. The CC1407P, hosted by an LP-MSPM0G5187, and an LP-CC1354P10-1 exchanged packets on 2-GFSK 50 kbps and all eight Wi-SUN FSK modes; the CC1354P10 received 20 of 20 from the CC1407P in every one. The CC1407P received 20 of 20 in seven of them, and lost packets in modes 4a and 5 in both runs. Pings were answered 20 of 20 both ways in five protocols and partly in four, with round trips of 7 to 19 ms, timed on the MSPM0 host from handing over the ping to the answer arriving, so SPI time and the other radio's turnaround are included. TI's two long-range modes, the CC1407P's XLR and the CC1354P10's SimpleLink Long Range, did not exchange a packet. The same connection showed the CC1407P receiving for the first time, carrier sense answering correctly at low thresholds although the driver header calls it not functional, and a live range test in Home Assistant.

Terms: [vocab](../vocab.md#sub-1-ghz-radio-protocols-cc1407p). Code, tables and evidence: [mspm0g5187/radio](../../mspm0g5187/radio/README.md#two-radios-the-cc1407p-against-a-cc1354p10) and [cc1354p10/radio-rx](../../cc1354p10/radio-rx/README.md#as-a-test-partner-for-the-cc1407p). Checked on an Intel Mac, macOS 15.7.9, TI Arm Clang 5.1.1.LTS, MSPM0 SDK 2.11.00.07, F2 SDK 8.33.00.16 with SysConfig 1.28.0, radio firmware 3.0.0.25.

**Question.** The CC1407P is a stand-alone sub-1 GHz transceiver; the CC1354P10 is a wireless MCU with its own sub-1 GHz radio. Anyone mixing them needs to know which protocols carry across.

**Vendor position.** TI lists Wi-SUN FSK modes 1a to 5 for both. TI does not say whether the two chips interoperate, and does not relate XLR to SimpleLink Long Range.

**What was tried.**

- Both boards run lab firmware. `radio_lab.c` on the G5187 and `partner.c` on the CC1354P10 take the same text commands: send, receive, ping, echo. `lab.py` on the Mac drives both and logs both serial ports.
- Eleven pairs, two runs. Nine protocols, plus XLR against two SimpleLink Long Range rates. Each pair gets 20 packets of 40 bytes each way, then 20 pings of 30 bytes each way. The ping is this repo's own `FRL PING` packet. The CC1354P10 used SysConfig's own setting for each protocol. Both radios reported every send as done.
- Ping turnaround changed between runs. In the first, a host command started the CC1407P's receive after each ping, and only the 50 kbps modes got answers. Suspected: the answer came first. In the second, the CC1407P's transmit chains to its receive on the radio itself.

**How far it got.** Verified on the boards, run 2. Run 1 agreed except where the ping method changed.

| Pair | CC1407P receives | CC1354P10 receives | Pings from CC1407P | Pings from CC1354P10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2-GFSK 50 kbps | 20 of 20 | 20 of 20 | 20 of 20, 17.9 ms | 20 of 20 |
| Wi-SUN 1a, 1b, 3, 4b | 20 of 20 each | 20 of 20 each | 20 of 20 each, 8.1 to 19.2 ms | 20 of 20 each |
| Wi-SUN 2a, 2b | 20 of 20 each | 20 of 20 each | 9 and 17 of 20 | 20 of 20 each |
| Wi-SUN 4a | 16 of 20 | 20 of 20 | 4 of 20 | 14 of 20 |
| Wi-SUN 5 | 6 of 20 | 20 of 20 | 11 of 20 | 3 of 20 |
| XLR against SimpleLink Long Range 5 and 2.5 kbps (two pairs) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Evidence: the DONE lines from both radios in both runs, and for run 2 each radio's send count and the packets the CC1354P10 printed. In run 2 the signal was about 5 dB lower from mode 2a on than in run 1.

**The line.** None for packets in the first three rows of the table. Three limits.

- **Modes 4a and 5, CC1354P10 to CC1407P.** The CC1407P's sync counter (packets whose start it found) equals its CRC counter (packets that passed the checksum), so the missed packets are never detected. The CC1354P10's setting names give 200 kbps with 50 kHz deviation (4a) and 300 kbps with 75 kHz (5); they were not compared with the CC1407P's register values.
- **Pings at 2a, 2b, 4a and 5.** Packets went 20 of 20 at 2a and 2b while pings from the CC1407P came back 9 and 17 of 20; pings from the CC1354P10 at mode 5 were 9 of 20 in run 1 and 3 in run 2.
- **XLR and SimpleLink Long Range.** The CC1407P decoded the header 7 and 16 times and passed no checksum; the CC1354P10 decoded nothing. They run at different rates (XLR 20 ksps with spreading 2; SimpleLink Long Range 5 and 2.5 kbps), and no TI document found pairs them.

**Ask.**

- **Open question:** which CC13xx settings interoperate with which CC1407P profiles?
- **For readers:** if you run modes 4a or 5 between these chips, report what you see.
